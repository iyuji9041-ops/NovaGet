This Privacy Policy explains how Zaswix (Package: com.zaswix.downloader) processes information and prioritizes user privacy and security.

1 100% On-Device Processing (No Central Servers)

Zaswix is built on a strict Zero-Knowledge architecture. The app does not operate any backend servers, proxy servers, tracking relays, or external cloud infrastructure. All parsing, extraction, downloading, and FFmpeg media conversion run 100% locally on your device's CPU using bundled Python 3.12 and yt-dlp binaries.

2 Zero Data Collection & Zero Telemetry

We believe your media consumption is your private business:

  • No Analytics SDKs: There are no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or third-party telemetry tools embedded in the app.
  • No Advertising IDs: We do not read or transmit your Google Advertising ID (AAID) or hardware identifiers.
  • No Logging: Your downloaded URLs, video titles, and search queries never leave your device.

⚠️ Important Account Safety Notice — Instagram & YouTube

DO NOT USE YOUR PRIMARY / PERSONAL INSTAGRAM ACCOUNT inside the app. If you need to access private reels, stories, or accounts you follow, always use a dedicated dummy or secondary account. Automating extraction scripts on personal social media accounts violates platform terms and can trigger automated security challenges, temporary action blocks, or account suspension by Meta/Instagram or Google/YouTube. Always browse anonymously as a guest whenever possible.

3 Ephemeral Session Cookies Policy

When you log into Instagram inside the built-in browser to view restricted reels:

  • Session cookies are temporarily held only in the Android WebKit CookieManager in private app storage.
  • When an extraction starts, cookies are exported to a temporary file instagram_cookies.txt.
  • Guaranteed Immediate Deletion: As soon as the download finishes, fails, or is cancelled, this cookie file is unconditionally deleted in a finally block.
  • Old leftover cookie files in the private cache are automatically purged every time Zaswix starts.
  • Cookie values and paths are strictly scrubbed and never printed to Android logcat or external logs.

4 Storage & Device Permissions

Zaswix requests only the absolute minimum permissions required to perform its functions:

  • Internet (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to open direct connections to the public media stream URLs that you request.
  • Storage (Scoped Storage): Used solely to write your downloaded video and audio files into your designated Downloads/Zaswix folder. The app cannot and does not read any of your personal photos, private documents, or other apps' files.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications: Used to keep long-running downloads active in the background and display live download progress, speed, and completion status.

5 Built-in Browser & AdBlocker

The integrated web browser connects directly to third-party services (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X). A 3-layer hybrid ad-blocker strips telemetry trackers and ads before they execute. Cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled (usesCleartextTraffic="false") to prevent man-in-the-middle tampering.

6 Legal Disclaimer

Zaswix is provided for personal backup, offline research, and fair-use archiving. Users are solely responsible for ensuring compliance with copyright laws and the terms of service of any third-party website.
